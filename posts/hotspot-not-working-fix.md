## Hotspot not working? Let’s fix it step by step

If your mobile hotspot is not working, you’re probably stuck without internet on your laptop or other devices.

This problem is very common — and the good news is, it’s usually easy to fix.

Let’s go step by step and solve it.

---

## 1. Turn Hotspot OFF and ON again

This is the simplest fix but works in many cases.

👉 Turn off hotspot  
👉 Wait 10 seconds  
👉 Turn it back ON  

Try connecting again.

---

## 2. Restart your phone

Temporary bugs can break hotspot functionality.

👉 Restart your phone  
👉 Turn hotspot ON again  

---

## 3. Check mobile data is ON

Hotspot won’t work without mobile data.

👉 Turn ON mobile data  
👉 Make sure internet is working on your phone  

---

## 4. Check data limit or plan

Sometimes hotspot stops due to:

- Data limit reached  
- Plan restrictions  

👉 Check your data usage  

---

## 5. Change hotspot name or password

Sometimes devices fail to reconnect.

👉 Change hotspot name (SSID)  
👉 Change password  
👉 Try again  

---

## 6. Forget and reconnect device

On your laptop or other device:

👉 Forget the WiFi network  
👉 Connect again manually  

---

## 7. Disable battery saver

Battery saver can turn off hotspot in the background.

👉 Turn OFF battery saver  
👉 Try hotspot again  

---

## 8. Reset network settings (last option)

If nothing works:

👉 Reset network settings  

⚠️ This removes saved WiFi and Bluetooth connections

---

## Quick summary

- Restart hotspot  
- Restart phone  
- Check mobile data  
- Change hotspot settings  
- Disable battery saver  

---

## Final tip

Hotspot issues are usually caused by small settings or temporary bugs.

Try each step patiently — one of these will fix your problem 👍
