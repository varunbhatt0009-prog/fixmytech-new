## WiFi connected but no internet? Don’t worry

If your WiFi shows connected but nothing loads, yeah… it’s really frustrating.

The good news?  
This is a very common issue — and in most cases, you can fix it yourself in a few minutes.

Let’s go step by step 👇

---

## 1. Restart your router (this fixes most cases)

Start with this — it works more often than you think.

- Turn off your router  
- Wait 30–60 seconds  
- Turn it back on  

This refreshes your connection and clears temporary bugs.

---

## 2. Check if internet is actually working

Before trying anything else:

👉 Connect another device to the same WiFi  

- If it also doesn’t work → problem is from ISP  
- If it works → issue is your device  

This saves a lot of time.

---

## 3. Forget and reconnect WiFi

This resets your connection properly.

- Go to WiFi settings  
- Tap your network  
- Click “Forget”  
- Connect again  

Simple step, but very effective.

---

## 4. Change DNS (powerful fix)

Sometimes DNS causes the issue.

Set DNS to:

- 8.8.8.8  
- 8.8.4.4  

This often fixes loading problems instantly.

---

## 5. Restart your phone or laptop

It sounds basic, but it works.

👉 Restart clears background network bugs  
👉 Helps reconnect properly  

---

## 6. Check airplane mode

Make sure:
- Airplane mode is OFF  
- WiFi is ON  

Sometimes it’s just a small setting issue.

---

## 7. Contact your internet provider

If nothing works:

👉 There might be an outage in your area  

Call your ISP and confirm.

---

## Quick summary

- Restart router  
- Check internet on another device  
- Reconnect WiFi  
- Change DNS  
- Restart device  

---

## FAQs

### Why does WiFi show connected but no internet?
Usually because of router issues, DNS problems, or ISP outage.

### Will restarting router fix it?
Yes — in most cases, this alone solves the issue.

### Is this a serious problem?
No, it’s very common and usually easy to fix.

---

## Final tip

Start with the router restart — it solves most cases.

If not, go step by step.  
One of these fixes will definitely work 👍
