## Why this problem happens

Sometimes your device connects to WiFi but cannot access the internet due to DNS issues, router problems, or ISP errors.

## Step 1: Restart your router

Turn off your router, wait for 30 seconds, and turn it back on. This refreshes your connection.

## Step 2: Forget and reconnect WiFi

Go to WiFi settings, forget the network, and connect again by entering the password.

## Step 3: Change DNS settings

Set DNS to:
8.8.8.8  
8.8.4.4  

This helps fix connection issues.

## Step 4: Check if internet is down

Try another device. If internet is not working anywhere, contact your service provider.

## Final solution

These steps solve most WiFi connection issues. Try each step one by one.
