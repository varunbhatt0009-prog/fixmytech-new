## Why mobile data is not working

Network settings or SIM issues may cause this.

## Step 1: Turn airplane mode ON/OFF
Resets network connection.

## Step 2: Check APN settings
Ensure correct network settings.

## Step 3: Restart phone
Refresh connection.

## Step 4: Reinsert SIM card
Fix SIM-related issues.

## Final solution
These steps solve most data problems.
