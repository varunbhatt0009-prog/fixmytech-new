## Mobile data not working? Try this

If your mobile data is ON but internet isn’t working, it’s really annoying.

Let’s fix it step by step 👇

---

## 1. Turn airplane mode ON and OFF

This refreshes network connection.

---

## 2. Restart your phone

Simple but very effective.

---

## 3. Check data balance

Make sure your data pack is active.

---

## 4. Enable mobile data properly

Check:
Settings → Mobile data → ON

---

## 5. Reset APN settings

Wrong APN can cause issues.

---

## 6. Contact your provider

If nothing works, network issue might be from their side.

---

## Final tip

In most cases, restarting or toggling airplane mode fixes it 👍
