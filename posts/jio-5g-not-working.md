## Jio 5G not working? Here’s what to do

If your phone supports 5G but it’s still not showing, yeah… it’s confusing.

Don’t worry — this happens to a lot of people, and it’s usually easy to fix.

Let’s go step by step 👇

---

## 1. Check if your area has 5G

First thing — make sure 5G is actually available in your area.

If 5G is not rolled out yet:
👉 Your phone won’t show it no matter what

---

## 2. Enable 5G in settings

Go to:
Settings → Mobile Network → Preferred network type  

👉 Select:
**5G / 4G / 3G / 2G (Auto)**

---

## 3. Turn on 5G in Jio settings

Open MyJio app → check if 5G is enabled  

Sometimes it’s not activated by default.

---

## 4. Restart your phone

Simple but works in many cases.

👉 Restart helps your phone reconnect to network properly

---

## 5. Update your phone software

Old software can block 5G support.

👉 Check for updates  
👉 Install latest version  

---

## 6. Reset network settings

If nothing works:

👉 Reset network settings  
(This will remove saved WiFi, so reconnect later)

---

## Quick summary

- Check 5G availability  
- Enable 5G settings  
- Restart phone  
- Update software  

---

## Final tip

In most cases, enabling 5G in settings or restarting fixes it.

Try these steps one by one 👍
