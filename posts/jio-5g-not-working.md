## Why Jio 5G is not working

Sometimes 5G may not work due to settings or network issues.

## Step 1: Check 5G coverage
Ensure your area supports Jio 5G.

## Step 2: Enable 5G in settings
Go to mobile network settings and select 5G/Auto.

## Step 3: Restart your phone
Restart to refresh network connection.

## Step 4: Update software
Install latest system updates.

## Final solution
These steps usually fix Jio 5G problems.
