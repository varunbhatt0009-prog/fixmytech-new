## Laptop running slow? Let’s fix it

If your laptop is taking forever to open apps or feels laggy, yeah… that’s frustrating.

The good thing is — you can speed it up without any technical skills.

---

## 1. Restart your laptop

This clears background processes and gives a fresh start.

---

## 2. Close unnecessary apps

Too many apps slow your system.

👉 Close apps you’re not using

---

## 3. Disable startup programs

Some apps start automatically and slow everything.

👉 Disable unnecessary startup apps

---

## 4. Delete temporary files

Junk files build up over time.

👉 Clear temp files to free space

---

## 5. Check storage space

If storage is almost full:
👉 Laptop becomes slow  

Try freeing up space.

---

## 6. Update your system

Updates fix bugs and improve performance.

---

## 7. Scan for viruses

Malware can slow down your laptop.

👉 Run a quick scan

---

## Final tip

Even simple steps like restarting and cleaning storage can make a big difference 👍
