## Why laptop becomes slow

Too many background apps and storage issues slow down performance.

## Step 1: Restart your laptop
Restart clears temporary memory.

## Step 2: Close background apps
End unnecessary tasks in Task Manager.

## Step 3: Free up storage
Delete unwanted files.

## Step 4: Disable startup programs
Reduce startup load.

## Final solution
These steps improve speed significantly.
